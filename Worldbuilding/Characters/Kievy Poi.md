**Kievy Poi** (195th day of 321 KP -- 312th day of 280 KP), known as **the Forefather of [[Magicians]]**, was a chemist who was unknown in his day, only rising to fame posthumously.

## Poi's Soil Experiment
Although many religious traditions attribute the origin of [[Magicians]] to the intervention of the [[God of Trickery]], historical scholars largely credits their emergence to an inadvertent outcome of one of Poi's underground chemical experiments.

Believing that the myth about [[Mana]] was rooted in science, Poi conducted various underground experiments to prove it's existence. The most significant of these was the **Soil Manipulation Experiment**, carried out in the hills of [[Tova]]. During this experiment, Poi  detonated a chemical device that spread a compound he believed to be mana, which it was not, but rather one that caused mana to violently react, which he could not visibly see. This detonation spread the compound through the soil of Tova similar to how a tree spreads its roots, but it did not have any immediate effect — rather it took **97–98 years** for it to activate.
The compound having caused the environment to gather dense mana made fetuses susceptible to absorbing enough of it to become conscious of it, which led to them attempt to manipulate it.

Now we don’t know where the myth of mana originated from, but scholars believe it to have been an early theory of atoms that was misinterpreted through the times, until Poi’s first interaction with the myth.
