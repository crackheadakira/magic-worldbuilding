Many believe the [[Magicians]] were sent by the [[God of Trickery]], but what one does not know is that they were the accidental byproduct of chemist Kievy Poi. Poi had believed that the myth about [[Mana]] was a true one, and in his search to prove it he did various underground experiments. The one that scholars attribute to being the catalyst was his soil manipulation experiment at the hills of [[Tova]]. He had detonated a chemical bomb that spread a compound he believed to be mana, which it was not, but rather one that caused mana to violently react, which he could not visibly see. This detonation spread the compound through the soil of Tova similar to how a tree spreads its roots, but it did not have any immediate effect — rather it took 97–98 years for it to activate.
The compound having caused the environment to gather dense mana made fetuses susceptible to absorbing enough of it to become conscious of it, which led to them attempt to manipulate it.
Now we don’t know where the myth of mana originated from, but scholars believe it to have been an early theory of atoms that was misinterpreted through the times, until Poi’s first interaction with the myth.
