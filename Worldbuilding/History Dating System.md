Kytyööäk plööyöö, or KP for short, stands for “time before [the] gods.” The era of gods is normally referred to as the one we live in currently. This period dating system was named by the blkytyamyusyöö, and the gods within the word KP is supposed to refer to them.

We are currently in the era kytyööäkn plöö, KPN, which stands for “time after [the] gods,” with the year being 612KPN.

*This system is similar to the BD & AD system. The world is currently at 3525 AD.*
