The **God of Origin** is the singular god many religious texts attribute to being the forefather of all beings and gods.
The earliest mention of him appears in verse 4:14 of the religious book *Fons et origo of Life*, where he is mentioned as having traveled to our solar system due to the Sun. He then saw that the planets within the solar system did not have life, but he envisioned there being life — which has led to interpretations of him being able to foresee the future. So he embraced the sun, whether that be metaphorically or physically is unknown. But the widely accepted understanding is that he physically embraced the sun to offer his own life to create life on Earth. The tearing of his limbs is believed to be a posthumous autonomous action performed by his will, after offering his own life to the Sun, rather than being torn by him.

But as he visited the solar system when there existed no life on Earth, scholars date his arrival ranging from a billion years ago to 3.5 billion years ago. This matters as in verse 5:21 from *Fons et origo of Life* it is mentioned that he will arrive again in two billion years to see how life has grown.
