**Blöökyty**, or mana, is the idea of an energy / particle that exists outside of the world, or stands above it. It was originally believed to be a simple folk-talke meant to explain how gods could wield their power, but in 993KP a few beings with blöökyty within them were born. These new beings could control the blöökyty they saw however they wished, but now to us their control was still primitive. By 965KP, all the beings had died out due to failing to comprehend that blöökyty also composed them, so many experienced death by their own hands by simply exhausting all the blöökyty in their surrounding area.

It took until 893KP when another batch of these beings would be born, and these beings had learned from the mistakes of their predecessors. By then the beings who could not carry or sense blöökyty were starting to become afraid of the blkytyamyusyöö and tried their best to fight against them. This war, which started on 847KP, was dubbed the Blkytyplt Öönplyöö — War [of the] Lesser [beings], and lasted until 840KP. This mocking title was given by the then plööblkytyamyusy (the deity of Magicians, a title inherited every 100 years by the next generation).

As [MANA] is an invisible particle many question how it regenerates, but the agreed consensus is it exists throughout the universe and is constantly moving, similar to air particles. So whenever a gap is left behind after a [MAGICIAN] has used [MANA] it is simply a matter of time until the gap is filled again, mostly dependent on how much [MANA] was expended from the surrounding area.

[MANA] can’t change physical states, but objects can contain denser amounts of [MANA]. After a certain amount of [MANA] it becomes toxic to anyone who consumes it — even [MAGICIAN]s.
[MANA] can be infused within technology due to the ideas of visionary Kytäk Plöö.
