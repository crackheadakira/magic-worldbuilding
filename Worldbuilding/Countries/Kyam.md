In 251 KPN, a blkytyamyus by the name of [[Kytäk Plöö]] came up with an idea on how to use magic in manufacturing. This idea was so revolutionary that the country of Kyam, that Kytäk resided in when he came up with this idea, become the number one super-power in under a year. This was due to Kyam’s ultimate combat power achieved by incorporating mana into warfare. They sent off the poor and disabled who were “equipped” with a now-technically-forbidden weapon, the Blkytyamyusäkkyty — the magician’s rain.

A soldier would be filled to the brim with mana, which a magician could activate remotely. This would cause the soldier’s body to explode unleashing the mana within. The mana would scatter through the air at a high-velocity, but due to being an “invisible” particle it wouldn’t push any objects. Instead it would move the particles of air alongside it until it had traveled a kilometer or more, depending on how much mana was injected into the soldier. The act of moving those particles of air removed all the oxygen within the surrounding soldiers and environment, meaning one would die nearly instantenously in excruciating pain. Imagine seeing a man blow up from within, releasing a harsh black toxin (a side effect of mana interacting with the blood system), and feeling the air be sucked out of every orifice.
